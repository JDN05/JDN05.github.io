Bob and the Magic Circle

The theme of the game jam was "LOOP". My partner and I decided to create a level with the layout of a Mobius strip, where the top of the level becomes the ground you step on as you keep going. It was my first time using Godot instead of Unity, so I had the extra challenge of learning a new game engine completely from scratch. I implemented concepts that I had not yet learned in Unity, such as tile maps, variable sprite animations, and more.

All assets and code were created by myself and my partner during this 96-hour game jam.
