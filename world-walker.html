World Walker

This was the first full game I ever made. I had been working with a player controller that allowed the player to spin themselves by bumping into walls and platforms in the right way, and I realized that it would be a fascinating game mechanic to have your rotation control the level. That idea spawned World Walker, where the platforms that appear in the level depend on how you position yourself.

Level design and most of the programming were completed by me as a group assignment a Global Online Academy course on Game Development.
